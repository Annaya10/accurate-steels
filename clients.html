Industries We Serve

Our fabrication and engineering capabilities span across the UAE's most critical industrial sectors.

Oil & Gas

Offshore platforms, pipeline supports, pressure vessels and process equipment for upstream and downstream operations.

Marine & Ports

Ship repair, hull fabrication, port infrastructure, mooring systems and marine piping for commercial and naval vessels.

Construction

Structural steel frameworks, mezzanine floors, warehouses and architectural metalwork for commercial and industrial buildings.

Petrochemical

Precision-machined components, custom flanges, valves and structural supports for refinery and petrochemical plants.

Infrastructure

Bridges, walkways, handrails and structural steel for public infrastructure and government development projects.
