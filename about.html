Our Legacy

Our Journey in Musaffah


Accurate Steel Fabrication EST was founded in 2015 in the heart of Musaffah Industrial Area, Abu Dhabi — the UAE's premier manufacturing hub. What began as a focused structural steel operation has grown into one of the region's most capable multi-discipline fabrication companies.

Today, we operate one of the most technologically advanced fabrication facilities in the region, serving the oil & gas, construction, marine, and infrastructure industries across the UAE and beyond.

QHSE Policy

Quality, Health, Safety & Environment

Our QHSE commitment is the foundation of every project we undertake — from the first weld to final delivery.

Quality

Built on ISO 9001:2015, our Quality Management System ensures zero-defect fabrication through 100% dimensional inspection, NDT testing on all structural welds, and full material traceability documentation for every project.

ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Health

The wellbeing of our workforce is non-negotiable. We enforce strict occupational health protocols, regular medical screenings, ergonomic workstation standards, and a zero-tolerance policy on unsafe practices across all sites.

Zero Incident Culture

Safety

Safety-first engineering is embedded in our culture. Full PPE compliance, daily toolbox talks, risk assessments before every task, and adherence to international HSE standards on every fabrication and installation site.

100% HSE Compliance

Environment

We are committed to minimizing our environmental footprint. Responsible waste management, controlled blasting and painting operations, and compliance with UAE environmental regulations are standard practice at our Musaffah facility.
