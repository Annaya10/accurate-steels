About Company


Accurate Steel Fabrication is situated in industrial area of Abu Dhabi Musaffah, which started its operation from 2015. Accurate is and ISO 9001:2015 certified company which takes pride in being the total solution provider for mechanical engineering and services. Workshop is equipped with a wide range of equipment from light to heavy duty machines required to undertake even the most exacting jobs with hands on expertise.

The Company is being managed by a dedicated team of highly qualified experienced professionals. It has built its reputation by its sheer commitment to quality and strict adherence to time schedules project after project.

A Message from
Our Founder & CEO

When I founded Accurate Steel Fabrication in 2015, my vision was simple: to build a company that the UAE's most demanding industries could rely on — not just for quality steel, but for engineering partnership. A decade later, that vision has become our identity.

Every project we take on is a reflection of our commitment to precision, safety, and on-time delivery. We've grown from a focused structural steel operation to one of the region's most capable multi-discipline fabrication companies — and we're just getting started.

Our team of 50+ certified engineers and fabricators brings decades of combined expertise to every weld, every cut, and every delivery. I'm proud of what we've built — and even more proud of the clients who trust us to build for them.
