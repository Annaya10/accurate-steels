Service 01

Fabrication & Erection

We have been involved in metal and stainless steel fabrication for the past 7 years. Accurate Steel Fabrication Est. provides custom drawing and fabrication services in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Musaffah UAE.

Our in-house engineering team handles everything from initial shop drawings and material procurement through to fabrication, surface treatment, and final on-site erection — giving you a single accountable partner for the entire project lifecycle.

  • Custom Shop DrawingsFull CAD/CAM design and shop drawing production to client specifications.
  • Metal & Stainless SteelCarbon steel, stainless steel (304, 316), and aluminium fabrication.
  • AWS D1.1 Certified WeldingMIG, TIG, and SMAW welding with NDT inspection and quality sign-off.
  • On-Site ErectionExperienced erection crews with full QHSE-managed site operations across the UAE.

Machining
