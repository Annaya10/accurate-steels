Engineering Challenges, Tailored Solutions

Structural Steel Abu Dhabi

Khalifa Port Crane Structures

Challenge

Client required heavy-duty crane support structures capable of withstanding high-salinity marine environments, with a compressed 8-week delivery window and zero tolerance for dimensional variance.

Our Solution

We fabricated the structures using marine-grade steel with SA 2.5 blasting and epoxy coating, deploying a dedicated team of AWS D1.1 certified welders. Full NDT testing was completed on-site, and delivery was achieved 5 days ahead of schedule.

Marine Grade Steel AWS D1.1 On-Time Delivery
CNC Machining Musaffah

Oilfield Valve Body Production

Challenge

A major oilfield operator needed 500+ precision valve bodies and actuator components to API 6A standards, with ±0.01mm tolerances and full material traceability documentation within 6 weeks.

Our Solution

Our 5-axis CNC machining center ran dual-shift operations with real-time quality checks at every stage. Full mill certificates and dimensional reports were provided for every batch, meeting API 6A requirements with zero rejections.

API 6A ±0.01mm Zero Rejections
Marine Engineering Dubai

Emergency Hull Repair — Cargo Vessel

Challenge

A 180m commercial cargo vessel sustained hull damage at Dubai dry dock, requiring emergency structural repair and class-approved certification within 72 hours to avoid costly port delays.

Our Solution

Our 24/7 marine response team mobilized within 4 hours. We completed cut-and-weld hull repairs, aluminum work on the superstructure, hydraulic system checks, and full blasting and painting — delivering class certification within 68 hours.

Emergency Response Class Approved 68-Hour Turnaround
